## Filter fixes

- Region/category/quarter/status options are populated from the selected year, not from the entire archive.
- Option counts reflect the other selected dropdown constraints and applied search/chips; zero-result options are disabled.
- The category menu and category chips replace one another rather than adding contradictory constraints. The same applies to region chips and the region menu.
- Mobile gaming events can also be discovered under Mobile / App Growth; explicitly tagged affiliate and adtech events are discoverable under those categories.
- A specific quarter includes only exact-date events. Month-only announcements stay in Dates pending; no exact day is invented.
- An active-filter summary and a specific empty-state explanation make restrictive combinations visible.
- Sources and date status remain visible on cards. Date conflicts are held, and unannounced series have no claimed future edition or future location.
