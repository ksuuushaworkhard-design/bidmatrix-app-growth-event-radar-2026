## Date conventions

- Event-wide date ranges are used, including organizer-listed conference/networking days.
- Quarter and month come from the start date.
- City is left blank when the reviewed source identifies a country/venue without a confirmed city; the card displays the country.
- SiGMA Eurasia retains the organizer's location wording, RAK – Dubai, pending more precise venue information.
- Affiliate Leaders Summit is separately listed because it has its own official event page; it is co-located with SBC Summit.
